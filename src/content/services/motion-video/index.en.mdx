## Why this matters

A good app is still hard to sell if people don't understand what it does. A screenshot shows how something looks, but not the flow: what gets tapped first, what happens next, and which problem gets solved. A short video of the product at work answers that faster than a page of explanation.

## What we deliver

We make product explainer and app demo videos from screen capture and animated interface. Because our team builds apps itself, we know which flows deserve to be shown and which can be mentioned in passing. The result is a video that is accurate to your product, not generic illustration that could belong to anyone.

The videos are built with Remotion, a framework that creates video from React code. Text, colors, and scene order live in project files that can be edited and re-rendered.

## How we work

We start with a simple question: who will watch this, and what should they understand when it ends? From there we write a short script and storyboard, record or prepare the app screens, then build the animation, text, and transitions. You review a draft before the final version is rendered.

Once the video is done, its project files stay reusable. If a feature changes in a later release, we update only the related scene.

## Ideal for

This service fits teams that already have a running app or prototype and need a quick way to show it to prospective users, partners, or stakeholders.
