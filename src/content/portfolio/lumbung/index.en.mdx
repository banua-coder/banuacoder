## Context

A lumbung is the Indonesian communal rice granary raised on stilts, where a
household or village stores its harvest together. The name fits because the
product is about storing together: one place for personal money, money shared
by two, and each person's share of it. It is a working name and not final.

This is our own product, not client work. It is in active development: the
site is live and takes waitlist sign-ups, but the app cannot be downloaded yet.
This page only describes what exists and marks what does not.

## What we are building

- **Flutter client**: a Melos workspace with clean architecture and
  Bloc/Cubit. Every entry is written to an encrypted local database first, so
  the app stays fully usable with no network.
- **Shared spaces**: one Workspace concept for personal, couple, family,
  housemate, group trip and small business use. People are invited with a
  role, not by sharing a password.
- **Sync**: changes sync per entry across devices with automatic conflict
  merge, not as a single whole-file backup.
- **Precise money**: amounts are stored as minor units, a currency code and a
  scale, so rupiah, yen and Kuwaiti dinar are not forced into one two-decimal
  rule.
- **Go backend**: a modular monolith with Echo, pgx, River for background
  jobs, and PostgreSQL. It handles sync, accounts and billing.
- **Marketing site and admin console**: a bilingual site with a waitlist
  (Nuxt 4, Tailwind v4), plus an admin console and a status page.

## Engineering notes

Offline-first here is not a fallback mode. The app writes to local storage
first and the network only delivers the result. That means sync has to merge
two devices that both changed data while disconnected, and that is where most
of the backend work goes.

The other principles we hold: calm language, with no gamification and no
guilt; data encrypted on the device; no scraping of users' bank accounts; and
data that can be exported at any time.

## Where it stands

Lumbung is not released and has no users yet. That is why this page carries no
user numbers, testimonials or app store links, and why the prices on the site
are still plans. Follow progress on the
[Lumbung site](https://lumbung.banuacoder.com).
